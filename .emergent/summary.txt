<analysis>
The previous AI engineer (E1) developed a modern record label website for 808Records. Initial work focused on a single-page React frontend, iteratively refined based on user feedback. The design evolved from rainbow gradients to a sleek black/white aesthetic with subtle gradients, interactive elements, custom scrollbars, and non-selectable text. The final phase involved implementing a full-stack admin system with FastAPI and MongoDB for authentication, allowing administrators to log in.
